## Why no children

These are scenes, materials and environments — **no identifiable children.**

Hive exists to stop children's photographs reaching people who have no right to
see them. Shipping real children's photos as demo data, in a public repository,
would contradict the product's entire premise. Worth stating in the report
rather than leaving as an unexplained absence.
